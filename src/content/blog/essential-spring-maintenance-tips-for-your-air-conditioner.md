As winter fades and warmer temperatures approach in Colorado Springs, it's the perfect time to prepare your air conditioning (AC) system for the upcoming cooling season. Proper spring maintenance can enhance efficiency, extend lifespan, and ensure reliable performance. Here are key steps to get your AC ready:

## Inspect and Replace Air Filters

Dirty filters restrict airflow, reducing efficiency and air quality. Check filters monthly and replace them if they're dirty or every 1-3 months, depending on usage.

## Clean the Condenser Coils

Over time, condenser coils can accumulate dirt and debris, hindering heat exchange. Gently clean them with a soft brush or cloth to maintain optimal performance.

## Clear the Condensate Drain

A clogged condensate drain can lead to water damage and increased humidity levels. Ensure the drain is free of obstructions by flushing it with a mixture of water and vinegar.

## Check Refrigerant Levels

Low refrigerant levels can cause inadequate cooling and strain the system. Have a professional technician assess and adjust refrigerant levels as needed.

## Examine Ductwork for Leaks

Leaky ducts can result in cooled air loss and higher energy bills. Inspect visible ductwork for leaks and seal any gaps with appropriate duct sealant.

## Test the Thermostat

Ensure your thermostat accurately reads temperatures and responds to adjustments. Consider upgrading to a programmable thermostat for enhanced control and energy savings.

## Lubricate Moving Parts

Components like fans and motors can benefit from lubrication, reducing wear and noise. Apply manufacturer-recommended lubricants to all moving parts.

## Schedule Professional Maintenance

An annual tune-up by a qualified technician can identify potential issues before they become major problems. Professionals can clean internal components, check system performance, and ensure everything operates safely.

By following these maintenance steps, you can enjoy a cool, comfortable summer in Colorado Springs while keeping energy costs in check. Remember, regular upkeep not only improves efficiency but also extends the lifespan of your AC system, providing reliable comfort for years to come.
