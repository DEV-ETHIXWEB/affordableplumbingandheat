As a homeowner in Colorado Springs, you've likely faced a leaky faucet, a running toilet, or a clogged drain. The first thought for many is to grab a wrench and tackle the job themselves. While the "DIY" approach can be tempting, knowing when to roll up your sleeves and when to call a professional can save you time, money, and a major headache.

Here’s a guide to help you decide whether to tackle that plumbing issue yourself or call the experts at Affordable Plumbing, Heat & Electrical.

## When to DIY: Simple Fixes You Can Handle

Minor plumbing issues are a great way to save a little money and gain confidence as a homeowner. These are typically problems that don't involve major pipes, water pressure, or complex systems.

### **1. Clogged Drains:**

- **The fix:** For simple clogs, a plunger or a basic drain snake can often do the trick. You can also try a mixture of baking soda and vinegar to clear minor blockages.
- **When to call a pro:** If the clog persists or you have multiple clogged drains in your home, it could be a sign of a deeper issue in your main sewer line. This is a job for a professional plumber with the right tools, like a hydro-jetter.

### **2. Leaky Faucets or Showerheads:**

- **The fix:** A persistent drip, drip, drip is often caused by a worn-out rubber washer or gasket. These are inexpensive parts that you can easily replace with a few basic tools.
- **When to call a pro:** If you've replaced the parts and the leak continues, there might be an issue with the valve or the fixture itself.

### **3. Running Toilets:**

- **The fix:** A toilet that won't stop running is usually a simple fix. Check the flapper valve, the chain, or the fill valve. Replacement kits are widely available at home improvement stores.
- **When to call a pro:** If you’ve replaced the components and the toilet is still running or you suspect a leak in the tank or bowl, it's best to call a plumber.

## When to Call a Professional Plumber

Some plumbing problems are simply too risky to handle on your own. A mistake can lead to costly water damage, mold growth, or even dangerous health hazards. When in doubt, it’s always best to call a professional plumber in Colorado Springs who is licensed, insured, and experienced.

### **1. No Hot Water:**

- **The problem:** If your water heater isn’t producing hot water, it could be a number of issues—a broken heating element, a faulty pilot light, or a mineral build-up inside the tank.
- **Why to call a pro:** Water heaters involve gas lines and/or high-voltage electricity, which can be extremely dangerous. A professional can diagnose the problem safely and recommend the best solution, whether it's a repair or a full replacement.

### **2. Low Water Pressure:**

- **The problem:** Low water pressure throughout your home could be a sign of a serious issue, such as a major leak, mineral build-up in your pipes, or a problem with your main water line.
- **Why to call a pro:** A plumber can accurately diagnose the source of the problem and address it without causing further damage to your home’s plumbing system.

### **3. Suspected Hidden Leaks:**

- **The problem:** A sudden increase in your water bill, a musty smell, or soft spots on your walls or floors could indicate a hidden leak behind a wall or under your foundation.
- **Why to call a pro:** Professional plumbers use advanced leak detection equipment to find the source of the leak and repair it before it causes extensive damage to your home’s structure.

### **4. Sewer Line Issues:**

- **The problem:** Gurgling noises from drains, foul odors, or sewage backing up into your sinks and toilets are clear signs of a main sewer line blockage.
- **Why to call a pro:** This is not a DIY job. Sewer lines are a complex system, and a professional plumber has the necessary equipment and expertise to clear the blockage and perform any necessary repairs.

### The Bottom Line

While a DIY approach can be rewarding for small tasks, the complex nature of your home’s plumbing system makes it crucial to know your limits. When a job is beyond a simple fix, calling a professional is the smartest, safest, and most cost-effective decision in the long run.
