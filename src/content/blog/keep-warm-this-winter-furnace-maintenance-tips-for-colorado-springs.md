## Your DIY Pre-Winter Furnace Checklist

These are the three things every homeowner in Colorado Springs should do *before* the constant cold sets in. They’re simple, fast, and will save you money.

### 1. Change That Dirty Air Filter (Seriously!)

If we could drill one thing into every customer's head, it’s this: **A dirty filter is the #1 reason for a furnace failure.**

When your filter is clogged with dust, pet hair, and debris, your furnace has to work harder to pull air through, which skyrockets your energy bill and often leads to the system overheating and shutting down.

- **Our Recommendation:** Check your filter monthly, and replace a standard fiberglass or pleated filter every **30 to 90 days** during the heating season. It’s the easiest step to boost your system's efficiency and **improve your indoor air quality**.

### 2. Clear All Vents and Air Returns

Your furnace could be running perfectly, but if the warm air can’t properly circulate, you’ll end up with cold spots and a system that runs longer than it should.

- **The Vents:** Make sure all supply registers (the ones blowing warm air) are fully open and completely unobstructed by furniture, rugs, or drapes.
- **The Returns:** The large, grated **return air vents** are crucial for the system to suck cold air back in for heating. Keep these clear!
- **The Benefit:** By maximizing airflow, you ensure efficient, even heating throughout your entire home.

### 3. Test Your Thermostat for Accurate Performance

Your thermostat is the brain. If it’s malfunctioning, the entire system is off.

- **The Quick Check:** Switch your thermostat to the "Heat" setting and raise the temperature a few degrees above the current room temperature. Listen for the furnace to kick on and confirm that warm air begins to flow.
- **Smart Thermostats:** If you have a smart or programmable unit, check your settings to ensure your heating schedule is correct. You don't want to be heating an empty house!
